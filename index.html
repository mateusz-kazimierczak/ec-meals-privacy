EC Meals Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

EC Meals is a meal management app for Ernescliff residents and authorized staff. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile app and its connected meal service. The developer is Mateusz Kazimierczak. For privacy or support questions, contact ernescliffapps@gmail.com.

Request account or data deletion

Information handled by the service

  • Account and profile information: username, name, account role and status, and, where supplied for your profile, email address, room number, birthday month and day, and dietary information. Accounts and profile details may be provided or managed by authorized Ernescliff administrators.
  • Meal information: weekly meal selections, packed meal choices, meal preferences, and records of changes to those selections.
  • Notification information: reminder preferences, selected schedules, and, when you register for push notifications, a push token and related device registration details. Email addresses are used for enabled email communications.
  • Operational records: timestamps, user identifiers, meal-change audit records, notification delivery results, and diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. Hosting and delivery providers may process IP addresses and technical request information.
  • Communications: information you send when contacting support or requesting changes to your account or data.

Why information is used

We use this information to authenticate users, maintain accounts, record meal selections, organize meal preparation, provide college activity updates, send communications according to your settings, investigate problems, and keep administrative and security records. We do not use app information for targeted advertising or sell personal information.

Storage, access, and service providers

The app communicates with the EC Meals backend, hosted on Vercel. Application accounts, meal selections, and preferences are stored in MongoDB Atlas. Firebase is used for Android notification delivery; it is not the app's account database or authentication service.

Authorized Ernescliff staff can access information needed to manage the meal service. Meal lists and administrative reports may include names, meal choices, and dietary details needed for preparation. Reports may be exported to documents or Google Sheets. If you use a device's share function, the destination you choose receives the shared document.

Providers used to operate the service include:

  • MongoDB Atlas and Vercel for database storage and backend hosting.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Apple notification services on iOS, for push notifications.
  • Resend for meal notification emails, and Mailchimp for college activity content and email communications when those features are used.
  • Google Sheets and BigQuery for meal administration, audit records, and notification delivery records.

These providers process the information needed to perform their functions. Their infrastructure may process data outside your country. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service or its users.

Device storage and security

The mobile app stores your sign-in session on your device so you can remain signed in. Exported reports may be stored temporarily in the app's cache. Production API requests use HTTPS, and account passwords are stored by the backend as password hashes. Access to service data depends on account permissions. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Notifications and your choices

You can change meal and notification preferences in the app. Push notifications require your device's permission; you can turn them off in the app or your device settings. Notification providers process push tokens and message delivery information. Logging out ends the app's saved sign-in session; it does not delete your server-side account or meal records.

Retention and account/data deletion requests

Account and meal data are retained while needed to provide the meal service and administer the associated account. Historical meal, audit, and delivery records may be retained when needed for service administration, security, record keeping, or legal obligations. Backups and provider logs can remain until their normal retention cycles expire.

You can request deletion of your EC Meals account and associated personal information, or request access or correction, by emailing ernescliffapps@gmail.com with the subject “EC Meals data request” and your username. Do not send your password. We may need to verify your identity before acting. For account deletion, we review the associated profile, meal and preference records, push registrations, and data held by service providers; we remove or de-identify information that is no longer needed and explain any records retained for the reasons above. There is no automatic 90-day deletion policy. Disabling an account alone is not a data deletion.

Intended audience

EC Meals is intended for Ernescliff residents and authorized staff aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information so we can review and address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version is published at this address with its effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate service communications.
